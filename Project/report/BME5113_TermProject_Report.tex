\begin{abstract}
\noindent This report addresses the two parts of the term project. \textbf{Part~1} implements the
Cobelli \emph{et~al.}\ (1982) intermediate-complexity glucose--insulin--glucagon model (Haefner
Ch.~12) and works through exercises 1--7: reproducing the IVGTT response, tracing where ingested
glucose goes, explaining the insulin ``hump'', and simulating diabetic, obese, hyperinsulinaemic and
insulin-overdose subjects. A central practical finding is that the literal Table~12.2 parameters do
not reproduce Fig.~12.4 and require a small, explicitly documented calibration. \textbf{Part~2}
implements the simplified Imperial College (``sIC'') HIV/AIDS compartment model (Haefner Ch.~15),
extends it with a waning vaccine (protected compartments $P_{f,2},P_{m,2}$, vaccination rate
$\nu=0.65\,$yr$^{-1}$, loss of protection $l=0.1\,$yr$^{-1}$), and studies the epidemic peak/decline,
cost, optimum vaccination rate, and vaccination versus condom use. Two modeling judgments are
required and documented: the literal progression rate $\gamma=1.16\,$yr$^{-1}$ gives $\Rzero<1$ and
must be corrected to a biologically grounded $\gamma=0.1\,$yr$^{-1}$ ($\Rzero\approx2.35$); and
protected individuals must remain in the frequency-dependent partner pool for the vaccine to express
herd immunity. All simulations are in Python (\texttt{scipy.integrate.solve\_ivp}); key results are
cross-checked with a second integrator.
\end{abstract}

\section{Reproducibility and code}
All models and analyses are in Python (NumPy/SciPy/Matplotlib). Part~1 lives in
\texttt{part1\_glucose/} (\texttt{cobelli.py} core; \texttt{ex1\_ivgtt.py}\,$\dots$\,\texttt{ex7\_icecream.py};
\texttt{verify\_core.py}); Part~2 in \texttt{part2\_hiv/} (\texttt{sic.py} core; \texttt{q\_a}\,$\dots$\,\texttt{q\_d}
analyses; \texttt{verify\_sic.py}). Each core ships a verification harness that checks the basal
steady state / conservation of individuals and agreement between the LSODA and Radau integrators.
Octave (\texttt{.m}) cross-check scripts for the two cores are provided in \texttt{octave\_crosscheck/}.
